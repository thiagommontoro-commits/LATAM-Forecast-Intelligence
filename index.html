Models vs. previous years — volume, accuracy and ranking by forecast year

How to read this table Each row is a forecasting model; the black row is the Blend (the final recommended number). Grey columns are actual sales of previous years, repeated on every row so each model can be compared line by line. For each forecast year you see: Volume (units), vs. previous year (growth), vs. last actual year, Accuracy (how close this model was to reality when it forecast the same horizon in past years — 100% = perfect) and Rank (1 = best model for that year). Each year has its own ranking because a model that is good at closing the current year is not always good at forecasting one or two years ahead. The coloured ★ marks the rank-1 model of each year. Use “Ranking year” in the filter bar to sort the table.

Track record — how accurate each model was in previous years

How to read this table We went back in time: at the August cutoff of each past year the model was trained only with data available at that moment and asked to forecast. The columns show how accurate it was for each past year. A model that is accurate every year (high minimum, small spread) is more trustworthy than one that was excellent once and poor in other years. Change “Ranking year” to see the track record for closing the current year (N), one year ahead (N+1) or two years ahead (N+2).

What is a “factor”? Anything a model uses to predict sales. There are two big groups: (1) Sales-based factors — the segment's own sales history (recent months, moving averages), the usual month-by-month seasonality and the long-term trend; and (2) External drivers — agricultural and economic indicators (crop prices and production, interest rates, exchange rate, credit, delinquency…), only those allowed by the agronomic filter. Importance is measured by a simple test: scramble one factor and see how much worse the forecast gets. The bigger the damage, the more the model depends on that factor. Pure time-series models (ETS, SARIMA, Theta, STL) rely 100% on sales history by design.
